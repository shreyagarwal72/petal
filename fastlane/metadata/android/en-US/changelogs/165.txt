### What's Changed in v1.6.5 🚀

#### 🎨 Settings & Subpages ExpressiveHeader Integration
* **App-Wide Settings Integration**: Updated `PetalSettingsScreen.kt` to dynamically host `ExpressiveHeader` across both main Settings overview and all category subpages (Appearance, Privacy, Search/Homepage, Display & Zoom, Data & Storage, Updater, About).
* **Clear Browsing Data Layout & Padding Fix**: Fixed double padding issue in `PetalDeleteScreen.kt` by removing redundant outer Scaffold padding wrappers, ensuring `ExpressiveHeader` aligns seamlessly with top status bars.

#### 👤 User Account Header Line Limits
* **Single Line Title & Subtitle**: Updated `ExpressiveHeader.kt` to support configurable `maxTitleLines` and `maxSubtitleLines` limits, enforcing exactly 1 single line for title ("User Profile & Accounts") and 1 single line for subheading ("Manage account, cloud sync & preferences") in `ChromeAccountSyncScreen.kt`.
