Core Principles

Engineered to be the anti-Chromium.

Most Android browsers are repackaged Chromium shells bundled with tracking services. Petal is an independent, native engineering achievement.

01 / ENGINE INDEPENDENCE

GeckoView 156 Quantum Pipeline

Petal does not delegate web rendering to the manufacturer's Android WebView. It bundles Mozilla's quantum engine with full multi-process isolation, native Firefox add-ons, and standards compliance.

Standalone engine · Firefox Add-ons (AMO) · Quantum multi-process
02 / ARCHITECTURAL PRIVACY

Zero Telemetry by Construction

Privacy is not a toggle in a submenu. Petal bundles zero analytics SDKs, zero telemetry endpoints, and zero cloud reporting beacons. Strict Enhanced Tracking Protection is active on first launch.

No Google Firebase · No Sentry · Isolated cookie jars

Under The Hood

Modular Multi-Process Architecture

Explore how Petal stitches together Mozilla GeckoView C++ runtime bindings with modern Jetpack Compose Kotlin layers.

Independent Quantum Core

GeckoView 156 Standalone Engine

Petal embeds Mozilla GeckoView 156 with an isolated quantum multi-process pipeline. It does not use the OEM Android WebView, preventing vendor delays and third-party tracking hooks. 

GeckoRuntime.create(context, GeckoRuntimeSettings.Builder()
  .aboutConfigEnabled(true)
  .contentBlocking(ContentBlocking.STRICT)
  .build())
