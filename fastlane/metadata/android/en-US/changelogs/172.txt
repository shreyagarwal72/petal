### What's Changed in v1.7.2 🚀

#### 🎨 Universal Expressive Header Support & Bookmark Padding Fix
* **Tab Manager Expressive Header**: Upgraded `PetalTabGridSwitcher.kt` (Tab View) to use `ExpressiveHeader`, adding translucent liquid glass chrome, active tab counters, and seamless navigation.
* **Bookmarks Header Padding Fix**: Resolved double status-bar inset padding on `PetalBookmarksScreen.kt`, aligning title spacing with History and Downloads pages.
* **Auxiliary Page Header Parity**: Upgraded Chrome Flags (`PetalChromeFlagsScreen.kt`) and Extensions (`PetalExtensionsScreen.kt`) to support `ExpressiveHeader`.
