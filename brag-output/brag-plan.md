# Brag Plan: Petal Browser

## What is this app?
Petal is an unapologetic, open-source Android browser built on Mozilla's GeckoView 157 engine with Material 3 Expressive UI, 35 dynamic polygon morphing shapes, and authentic zero telemetry.

## The angle
The anti-Chromium manifesto. In a mobile landscape swamped by Chromium clones full of diagnostic trackers and bloat, Petal arrives with genuine Gecko quantum multi-process isolation, mathematical spring physics, and zero telemetry by construction.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big bold typography: "YOUR BROWSER IS A CHROMIUM SHELL." A striking split card shatters/morphs into the Petal flower shape with GeckoView 157 highlighted.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **GeckoView 157 Quantum multi-process core**: Zero Chromium monopoly, true desktop-grade standards & AMO WebExtensions.
- **35 Material 3 Expressive shapes**: Dynamic morphing polygon geometry (spring damping 0.75f) with wallpaper-adaptive dynamic color.
- **Wavy seekbar media player**: Sine-wave ripples live during video playback, snapping to a calm flatline when paused.
- **Zero telemetry by construction**: No Firebase, no Sentry, isolated cookie jars.

## Outro / punchline
"Respects your attention, privacy, and device."
Petal — Download signed APK on GitHub.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Launch Petal → smooth adaptive M3 Expressive morph & auto-palette match.
2. **Key Action**: Load media or webpage → active sine-wave seekbar ripples, tracking shields lock down 12 trackers.
3. **Result**: Silky smooth quantum browsing with zero telemetry pings.
