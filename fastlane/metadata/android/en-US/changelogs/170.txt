### What's Changed in v1.7.0 🚀

#### 📳 Haptic Engine & Accessibility Overhaul
* **Universal Compose Tap Integration**: Created `PetalHapticFeedback` bound to `LocalHapticFeedback` in `PetalTheme.kt`, ensuring every standard button, card, chip, switch, and tap across Petal triggers tactile feedback seamlessly on every successful press.
* **Accessibility Connection**: Linked `PetalHapticEngine` directly to system accessibility Touch Exploration (`TalkBack`) and OS `HAPTIC_FEEDBACK_ENABLED` settings, guaranteeing rich haptic response for visually impaired users.
* **Instant Pattern Switching in Settings**: Added explicit vibrator cancellation prior to triggering new haptic patterns in `PetalHapticEngine.java`, ensuring pattern test chips in Settings trigger immediately without lag or overlap.
* **Multi-Tier Hardware Fallbacks**: Built distinct custom waveform and single-shot fallback vibrations for devices lacking API 30+ hardware primitive composition.
